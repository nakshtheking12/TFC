The Founders' Collective (TFC) is Bal Bharati Public School, Pitampura's official Entrepreneurship Club. Started by students who were tired of business being taught only in textbooks.

We believe entrepreneurship is a life skill — problem solving, storytelling, building, leading. Our motto: Beyond The Pitch — execution > slides.
